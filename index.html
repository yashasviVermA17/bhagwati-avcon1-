Frequently Asked Questions

01What types of HDPE pipes do you offer?
We offer a comprehensive range of HDPE pipes for potable water supply, irrigation, sewerage, and drainage applications. Available in various diameters and pressure ratings to suit different project requirements.
02What is the difference between laminated and unlaminated PP woven fabric?
Laminated PP woven fabric has a coating layer that provides moisture resistance and enhanced barrier properties, making it ideal for packaging hygroscopic materials. Unlaminated fabric offers breathability and is suitable for general packaging applications where moisture barrier is not critical.
03Do you supply products for bulk orders across India?
Yes, we have a robust pan-India supply network and can cater to bulk orders of any scale. Our logistics team ensures timely delivery to project sites across all states. We serve infrastructure companies, agricultural projects, and industrial clients nationwide.
04Are your products quality certified?
All our products are sourced from reputed manufacturers and undergo strict quality checks to ensure they meet industry standards. We are a GST-registered and TrustSEAL-verified supplier committed to delivering only premium-grade materials to our clients.
