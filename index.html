PP Tape Extrusion Line

Our production facility uses an advanced extrusion line that melts raw polymer granules at controlled high temperatures and converts them into thin, high-strength PP tape. The entire process is fully automated — from extrusion through stretching, annealing and winding — with precise temperature and speed control maintained at every stage.

The tape produced on this line delivers high tensile strength, uniform thickness and enhanced durability, forming the base material for our PP Woven Bags. Integrated dust collection and material-handling systems ensure both production efficiency and consistent quality throughout.

This advanced technology helps Bhagwati Avcon maintain industry-leading manufacturing standards across its product range.

High-Performance Circular Loom

The advanced circular loom used at our facility weaves PP tape into strong, high-quality woven fabric. Hundreds of shuttles rotate in perfect synchronization, ensuring uniform weaving density and consistent fabric strength across every batch.

This precision-engineered design enables high-speed production without compromising on quality. The resulting woven fabric forms the base material for our PP Woven Small Bags and Jumbo Bags.

This modern weaving technology allows Bhagwati Avcon to maintain high production capacity, superior fabric strength and consistent quality across its product range.

PP Tape Manufacturing & Weaving Unit

Our manufacturing facility produces high-quality PP (Polypropylene) tape using advanced, modern machinery. Raw material is processed into strong, durable PP tape and wound into large rolls, which then feeds into the production of PP Woven Bags and Jumbo Bags.

Hundreds of bobbins are organized on structured racks across the production floor, ensuring a continuous and uninterrupted weaving process. Quality is monitored closely at every stage — tape thickness, width and strength are checked against defined standards.

This modern infrastructure enables Bhagwati Avcon to manufacture PP Woven Small Bags and Jumbo Bags with high capacity and consistent quality, serving the packaging needs of industries across India.
